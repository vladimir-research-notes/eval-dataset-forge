Split construction

A split is a modelling decision, not a coin flip. Each strategy encodes an assumption about how the benchmark will be used; the builder records a reproducible SplitSpec (strategy, seed, ratios, group key).

strategyassumption / usetradeoff
randomi.i.d. examplesleaks near-dups / grouped items
stratified · familyrepresentative task mixneeds enough per family
stratified · difficultyequal difficulty across splitsdepends on the difficulty proxy
source-groupedno provenance leakagecoarser control of ratios
adversarialnear-dup clusters kept out of testhardest, most honest test set
temporalfuture-data / distribution shiftneeds trustworthy timestamps

Post-construction checks

After building, the tool re-runs leakage checks across the produced splits and reports split sizes/ratios, task-family / difficulty / source distributions per split, and any residual cross-split leakage (should be zero for grouped/adversarial strategies).

Stratification is best-effort at small N: exact proportions may be unreachable, so the builder reports the achieved distribution rather than silently forcing it. Missing timestamps fall back to insertion order with a warning.
