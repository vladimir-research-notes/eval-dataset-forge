Roadmap & research path

Where the project can go as a research contribution. These are open questions, not solved problems.

Integration contracts (Milestone 13)

eval-dataset-forge is designed to be a reusable dataset-quality layer. It will define (without depending on) integration contracts for:

  • code-agent-runtime — audit agent-task benchmarks before they gate a run.
  • BioAgentBench — audit synthetic biomedical evidence tasks.
  • LLM regression / observability — flag when a regression suite is too small or leaky to rank models.
  • scientific-claim-verifier — audit claim/evidence datasets for label conflicts and leakage.
  • agent-control-bench — audit control/safety task sets.

Research directions (docs/PAPER_PATH.md)

  • A standardized reliability profile for benchmarks and its distribution across public datasets.
  • Leakage → ranking-change experiments: how much reported progress is a dataset artifact?
  • Difficulty calibration against real response data (IRT-style), validating the cheap proxy.
  • Ranking stability as a first-class release statistic (a required-N stopping rule).
  • Optional, clearly-bounded semantic dedup/contamination mode, with a cost/coverage study vs. the lexical methods.
What would make this credible: reproducible pipelines and released audit artifacts for every claim, ablations showing each metric adds signal, and honest false-positive/negative reporting. No leaderboards, no "clean" certifications.
