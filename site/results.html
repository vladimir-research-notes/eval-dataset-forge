Results

The smoke audit runs the entire pipeline over every fixture, fully offline. Because the fixtures are hand-authored with deliberate defects, the audit's job is to rediscover the planted defects — that is the demonstration that the checks work.

Expected findings per fixture

fixtureplanted defects the audit should surface
qa_toyduplicate + paraphrased questions, mislabeled answers, ambiguous questions, train/test overlap
agent_tasks_toynear-duplicate tasks, leaked solutions, ambiguous scoring
retrieval_toyduplicated docs, query–document leakage, incomplete relevance labels
bio_toyentity-label conflicts, evidence–label mismatches
safety_toyover-/under-refusal and ambiguous-boundary label conflicts

Once Milestone 12 runs, this page reports the actual per-fixture counts (duplicates, leakage, label warnings, PII), difficulty distributions, the built release, the generated dataset card, and the bootstrap / ranking-stability demo — with any small-N instability warnings.

PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m eval_dataset_forge smoke --out results/reports/smoke_audit.md
