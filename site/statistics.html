Statistical uncertainty & ranking stability

A single accuracy number without an uncertainty estimate invites over-reading. We quantify how much a benchmark's verdicts can be trusted, using transparent resampling.

Methods

methodquestion it answers
Bootstrap CIhow wide is the interval around a system's score? (percentile CI, default B=1000, fixed seed)
Paired comparisonresampling the same examples, how often does A beat B, and what is the CI on the difference?
Ranking stabilityhow often does the top system / full ordering survive resampling and example removal?
Small-N warningis the dataset too small (or the effect too small vs. CI width) to rank these systems?

Inputs are per-example scores for one or more systems — synthetic fixture scores in the offline demo, real eval outputs in use. Emitted as BootstrapResults plus ranking-frequency summaries.

Deliberate non-overclaiming: we report percentile bootstrap intervals and resampling frequencies — simple, inspectable quantities. We do not claim exact coverage or apply corrections we cannot justify on this data. These methods quantify sampling uncertainty over the given examples, not whether those examples are representative.
