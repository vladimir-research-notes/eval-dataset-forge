Label audit & adjudication

Label noise caps any measurable accuracy and biases system comparisons. The audit surfaces defects and queues ambiguous cases for a human; it never rewrites a label.

Inter-annotator disagreement

When examples carry multiple annotator labels, we report simple, hard-to- misread quantities: raw agreement rate and per-example disagreement flags. Agreement coefficients (kappa) are a candidate future addition.

Adjudication queue

Flagged examples are exported to a deterministic, diff-friendly adjudication_queue.jsonl with the reason and conflicting values, ready for a human — or a future, disabled-by-default LLM-assisted adjudicator.

Ambiguity detection is heuristic: it both misses subtle ambiguity and over-flags terse-but-correct answers. Every flag is advisory; semantic conflict detection is out of scope offline.
